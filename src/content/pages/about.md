## What I do

I work on web application penetration testing and bug bounty hunting, with a focus on:

- **Web Security** — SQLi, XSS, SSRF, CSRF, IDOR, Broken Access Control, Clickjacking, Subdomain Takeover, Information Disclosure
- **API Testing** — authentication flows, logic vulnerabilities, access control
- **Cryptography & Reverse Engineering**
- **Red Teaming & Pwn**

Most of my practice happens on Hack The Box, TryHackMe, and PortSwigger Academy, alongside live bug bounty programs.

## Why this site exists

I write up the machines I solve, the bugs I find, and the things I learn along the way — partly as notes for myself, partly because writing something down properly is the best way to actually understand it.
